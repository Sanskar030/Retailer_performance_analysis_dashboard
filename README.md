Retailer Sales Dashboard — Excel Project
Built an interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 2 years (2022–2023) of retailer sales data across 6 retailers, 5 U.S. regions, and 6 beverage brands,
tracking Units Sold, Total Sales, Operating Profit, and Operating Margin. Structured the dataset as an Excel Table and used 9 PivotTables with 12 PivotCharts 
(bar, line, and pie visualizations) plus slicers to enable dynamic filtering and comparison of sales performance by brand, retailer, and region.
Tools use: Excel Tables, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Formulas, Interactive Dashboard Design
